## What It Does

Humanize AI Text skill rewrites AI-generated content to sound like it was written by a real human. It removes generic phrasing, adds natural variations, fixes robotic tone, and makes content undetectable by most AI detectors. Perfect for content creators, marketers, and writers who want to use AI to draft but publish human-sounding content.

## How to Install

```bash
clawhub install humanize-ai-text
```

## Key Capabilities

- Rewrite AI content to sound natural and human
- Remove generic AI phrasing and robotic tone
- Add natural variations in sentence structure and word choice
- Preserve original meaning and key points
- Undetectable by most AI content detectors
- Adjust formality: casual, professional, academic, creative
